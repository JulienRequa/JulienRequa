### Hi, I'm Julien 👋
Student at Phillips Academy Andover (Class of 2029), interested in physics, astronomy, and engineering.

**🔭 Current project: [exoplanet-search](https://github.com/JulienRequa/exoplanet-search)**
A vetting pipeline that decides whether a TESS planet candidate is a real planet or a false positive.
- Validated with a frozen-rules blind test (92% of real planets kept, 51% of false positives caught)
- Re-vetted all 2,896 open TESS candidates in its tested range
- Results archived on Zenodo: [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24571362](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24571362)

**🛠️ In progress**
- 📝 Paper: *Blind-tested vetting of open TESS planet candidates* (in preparation)
- 🪐 Hidden-planet search: looking for additional, smaller planets in known TESS systems using multi-sector data
- 🌀 Vortex-shedding energy harvester (fluid dynamics)
- 🛰️ Reaction-wheel attitude control (aerospace)

Tools: Python, NumPy, SciPy, lightkurve, batman
